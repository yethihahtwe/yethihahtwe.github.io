I miss my home book shelves. It’s been so long I haven’t seen my books. There were lots of items I’ve collected with a purpose to read later in life, and those that need to be read repeatedly like my Murakami collection.  
People would say I’m lucky as I don’t have a child left behind but there are books I usually feel like they are my children.  
That’s a lot of pain to leave the country. And so the books.
